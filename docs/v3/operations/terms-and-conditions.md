| Component | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| Rental fee | Charged for your rental period only (the dates you actually use the gear) |
| Deposit | 25% of the kit's current MSRP (manufacturer's suggested retail price) |
| Buffer days | 1 free day before and 1 after the rental period for pickup/dropoff and data transfer — not charged |

All pricing is shown upfront. No hidden charges.

### Deposit

- Secures your booking and covers potential damage, late returns, or loss

### Cancellation

| When you cancel | Deposit refund |
|-----------------|---------------|
| Within 24 hours of booking | 90% |

| Less than 1 week before rental dates | 0% |
| We cancel (gear unavailable, prior damage, etc.) | 100% |

## 3. Pickup & Return

### Pickup

- After booking confirmation, we'll reach out via WhatsApp to agree on a collection time and location
- The pickup window is 30 minutes
- Both parties must be present
- We walk you through all equipment, confirm condition together, and get your sign-off
- Tutorial provided if needed
- If you're absent without notice: treated as a cancellation (Section 2 cancellation policy applies)

### Return

- Same process — we agree on a return time and location within your buffer day
- We go through all equipment together and sign off on condition
- All original items must be accounted for
- Transfer your footage before return — we format memory cards
- If items are damaged beyond acceptable wear, we invoke the damage policy (Section 5)
- If you're absent without notice: treated as a late return (Section 4)

### Inspection & Documentation

We photograph/video gear before and after every rental — non-negotiable. Customer signs off on outgoing condition at pickup. Return inspection done together where possible. These photos and condition reports are the basis for any damage assessment.

## 5. Damage Policy

### How We Assess Damage

Every item is evaluated on two aspects:

| Aspect | Question |
|--------|----------|
| Functional | Does it still work as intended? |
| Physical | Is there visible damage? |

> **Open question:** Should we add a third aspect — intrinsic value (does this affect resale value)? To be decided.

### Physical Damage Scale

We use a 1–6 scale for physical damage:

| Level | Description | Who pays |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| 1–3 | Acceptable wear | No charge |
| 4–6 | Unacceptable damage | Customer charged (for now we will require 100% coverage from customer for level 6) |

> **Action item:** Define what each level looks like with photo examples before first rental. This is the visual guide referenced in the playbook.

We will also maintain a list of common scenarios and how we handle them (e.g. sand scratches from beach use = covered, dropped camera on concrete = case-by-case).

### What You Pay for Damage

| Item type | Minor damage (adventurous use) | Major / total / malicious damage |
|-----------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------|
| Expendable parts (lens guard, dive case, etc.) | 50% of replacement MSRP | 50% of replacement MSRP |
| Main item (camera, core equipment) | Depends on damage levels | 100% customer responsibility |

All replacement values are based on the item's current MSRP at the time of the damage assessment.

### Your Responsibilities During the Rental

- Use gear for its intended purpose
- Don't lend it to someone else
- Don't modify or disassemble the gear
- Keep it secure — don't leave it unattended
- Report any issues or damage immediately (or soonest possible) via WhatsApp with photo evidence
- Follow any activity-specific guidance provided at pickup

### Dispute Process

1. At return: preliminary inspection together (photos taken)
2. We then do a detailed inspection and maintenance check
3. If damage is found, we send you our case:
   - Before/after photos
   - Visual standards referenced (damage scale level)
   - Our assessment: acceptable or unacceptable, and why
4. We collect your explanation of what happened
5. We decide and document the resolution
6. Invoice sent: damage cost minus anything we absorb minus your deposit = what you owe (if anything)

Deposit is always applied first. You only pay the remainder.

## 6. Lost or Stolen Gear

Lost and stolen gear are treated the same — the gear is gone and you had custody.

| Situation | What you pay | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|-------|
| Lost (no police report) | 100% of current MSRP | You had custody, you're responsible |
| Stolen (with police report) | 70% of current MSRP | Police report required for any theft claim — for our records and future insurance claims |
| Unreturned (past late return threshold, Section 4) | 100% of current MSRP | We may also file a police report for theft by conversion |

- Replacement cost = current MSRP of the item at the time of loss
- Deposit is applied first, remaining balance invoiced to you (14-day payment window)
- A police report is required for any theft claim — this creates an official record and is needed for insurance purposes (Phase 2)
- A police report does not reduce your liability, but failure to file one when claiming theft will be noted

## Ideas (Post-MVP / To Explore)

### Optional Damage Waiver

Offer customers an optional add-on fee that caps their damage liability.

**Concept:**
- Customer pays an additional fee per day (e.g. RM 15–25/day or ~15% of rental fee)
- In return, their liability for accidental damage during normal use is capped (e.g. max RM 200 instead of full repair/replacement cost)
- Does NOT cover: theft, loss, negligence, unauthorized use, water damage from improper dive housing use

**Why:**
- Additional revenue stream
- Reduces friction for risk-averse customers
- Stepping stone to Phase 2 insurance-backed model

> **Important:** This needs proper calculation before implementation. Need to model: expected damage rates, average repair costs, pricing that covers the risk while remaining attractive to customers. Don't launch this until we have real damage data from the first few months of operation.

### Maximum Rental Period

Consider capping rental duration (e.g. 7–14 days) with extensions subject to availability and approval. At 2 kits, a single 30-day booking could be operationally devastating.
